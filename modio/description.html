Bank Layout puts every item in your bank into a planned tab, and makes new items land in the right tab too. No more dropping loot into tab 1 and sorting it by hand.

What it does

  • Moves every item into its planned tab, using the game's own "Move items to new tab".
  • Sets the game's own default tab for every item, so items you get later, including ones you've never had, land in the right tab by themselves.
  • Sorts each tab with the game's Default sort order (optional).
  • Asks before it first moves anything on a character.
  • No gameplay changes. Items you move by hand stay where you put them; the mod only re-applies when you buy a tab or the plan changes, or when you press Apply now.

The built-in plan

An opinionated 27-tab layout, generated from the game's own item data (equipment slot, combat style, item type, owning skill, upgrade chains) for all 3,710 bankable items in v1.3.1, expansions included:

  1. Weapons: Melee
  2. Armour: Melee
  3. Weapons: Ranged
  4. Ammo & Fletching
  5. Armour: Ranged
  6. Weapons: Magic
  7. Armour: Magic
  8. Jewelry & Gem Slot
  9. Capes
  10. Consumables & Prayer
  11. Effect Gear
  12. Woodcutting & Firemaking
  13. Fishing
  14. Cooking
  15. Mining & Smithing
  16. Farming
  17. Crafting Materials
  18. Runecrafting
  19. Herblore
  20. Summoning
  21. Archaeology & Cartography
  22. Harvesting
  23. Tokens & Stardust
  24. Chests, Keys & Clues
  25. Parts & Components
  26. Curios & Lore
  27. Events & Novelties

Fewer than 27 tabs? Planned tabs past your last one wrap back to the start: with 12 tabs, Fishing (13) shares tab 1 with Weapons: Melee. Buy a tab and the layout re-applies by itself.

Use your own plan

Don't like the built-in layout? In Mod Settings → Bank Layout → Your own plan:

  • Use my bank as the plan: arrange your bank by hand, then save that as the plan in one click.
  • Export plan: get the plan as a spreadsheet (CSV) with item names, change the tab numbers, and Import plan it back.
  • Change just a few items on top of the built-in plan.

Each character keeps its plan in its save, so it follows you to any device. Full guide, with the file format and examples: Using your own bank plan.

Good to know

  • Items the plan doesn't cover, such as other mods' items, are left alone.
  • With the bank's Custom sort order, items still move to their tabs but aren't re-sorted inside them.
  • Removing the mod leaves your bank as it is: the same as if you had moved everything by hand.
  • Export your save before the first apply if you'd like a way back.
